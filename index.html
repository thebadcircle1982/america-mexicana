Mexican and Mexican American life in the United States

The Border Crossed Us

In 1848, the U.S.–Mexico border moved. Some Mexican families came under U.S. rule without leaving their homes. This story begins there, on lands that were also home to Indigenous nations long before either country existed.

Start with the numbers

02 / Population by origin

More Than Half

In 2024, people of Mexican origin made up more than half of the U.S. Latino population. That includes people born in the United States as well as immigrants.

03 / Population growth

Growing Across Generations

Most of the growth in the Mexican-origin population between 2000 and 2021 was among people born in the United States.
